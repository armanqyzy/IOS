# USA memories 🇺🇸

**Lab 6 · Favorites Roulette** by Armankyzy Anara aka anarittto

A single screen IOS app with postcards from 10 places I visited in USA
Every tap on **Next stop** shows a random postcard (a photo of the place,
small palaroid photo of me there, title and a short description)
The same place never appears twice in a row.

**Category:** places in the USA where I was
(San Francisco, Ocean City, Washington DC, New York City, Niagara Falls,
Las Vegas, Los Angeles, Grand Canyon, Rehoboth Beach, Assateague island)
